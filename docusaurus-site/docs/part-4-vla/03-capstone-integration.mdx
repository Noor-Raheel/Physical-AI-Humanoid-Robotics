### Learning Objectives:
*   Assemble the full VLA pipeline.

### Overview

This culminating chapter brings together all the concepts and systems developed throughout the course into a single, autonomous Vision-Language-Action (VLA) pipeline. You will integrate the ROS 2 nodes for voice transcription, LLM-based planning, and robot control to achieve a complex task: commanding the robot to "pick up the red cube" using natural language.
